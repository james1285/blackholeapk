Why BlackHole?

Engineered For The Ultimate Listening Experience

Everything you love about modern music streaming services, combined with the unrestricted freedom of open source.

Lossless 320kbps Audio

Stream master-quality audio directly from official media streams at bitrates up to 320kbps AAC, delivering pristine acoustics, rich bass, and crystal-clear vocals.

Synchronized Live Lyrics

Sing along with time-synced, scrolling lyrics powered by Lrclib and community engines. Enjoy interactive karaoke-style line highlighting in real-time.

Offline High-Bitrate Downloads

Save tracks, albums, and entire playlists directly to local phone storage with complete ID3 tags, artist metadata, and high-resolution embedded album artwork.

Universal Playlist Import

Effortlessly import your favorite playlists from Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, and Resso. Just paste the share URL and enjoy your curated collection instantly.

10-Band Pro Equalizer

Fine-tune every frequency. Boost low-end sub-bass, enhance vocal clarity, or choose tailored presets for Electronic, Acoustic, Rock, Classical, and Hip-Hop.

Zero Advertisements & Open Source

Never suffer audio interruptions, sponsored popups, or paid paywalls. Open source under GPL v3.0 with no background telemetry or data monetization.

BlackHole is an ad-free, open-source music player application developed using Flutter by Ankit Sangwan.
